## Key Takeaways
1. for loops combine initialization, condition, and update.
2. while checks the condition before executing its body.
3. do...while executes its body at least once.
4. break exits the nearest enclosing loop.
5. continue skips the current iteration.
6. Nested loops can multiply the number of operations.
7. Accumulators maintain running results such as sums.
8. Loop conditions and updates must be checked carefully.
